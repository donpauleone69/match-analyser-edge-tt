# Product ideas

Parked ideas to review with Paul after his current reviews. One line each, with the source and the date. Not agreed scope.

| Idea | Source | Added |
|------|--------|-------|
| Use dead time in the video (set breaks of about a minute with the coach, the one timeout per match, optional towel breaks every 6 points) for something useful, such as coach notes or general set notes. Working out what is worth entering there is the valuable part. | Paul | 2026-11-17 |
| Zoom and position the video (pinch and drag in the frame), saved per video for playback, to fix badly cropped footage. Quality of life; cropping beforehand is a workaround. | Paul | 2026-11-17 |
| Matches are streaky: show stats per streak (runs of points won or lost) to see what changes during a run. | Paul | 2026-11-17 |
| Continuation phase question: loop the untagged middle shots of a long rally (for example shots 4 to 8) and ask one general question, such as "was there an attack in here" (by you, by them, none). Fills the blank middle of 8+ shot rallies and strengthens the opener and "turned" facts. | Paul | 2026-11-17 |
| A second tagging method based on phases instead of shots: watch each phase of the rally (opening, continuation, ending) and answer general questions about it. Experiment later as a separate mode. | Paul | 2026-11-17 |
| Show rally phases on the Rally Row as shots are tagged (for example serve, pre-attack, opening attack, continuation, point end; phases still to be defined). Last question of the rally: "Mark a section for review or a playlist?" so the tagger picks what is worth watching again. One tap for a lot of insight. | Paul | 2026-11-17 |
| Filming framing guide: when recording a match, show a guide so the table sits in the middle of the frame. | Paul | 2026-11-18 |
| After each rally, the one natural moment to look away from the video: room for a little more shot detail there. | Paul | 2026-11-18 |
| Shot redirect as a clean, low-fuss way to indicate placement. | Paul | 2026-11-18 |
| Serve recovery positioning (where the server recovers to after serving) as an analysis point. Capture with two taps: one at serve contact and one when the server is back in position (the moment their feet hit the ground), giving recovery time. | Paul | 2026-11-18 |
| Left-handed players: corner pad sides (BH left, FH right) and serve landing wing must flip for a left-hander. Later. | Paul | 2026-11-18 |
| Shot direction and redirect: wing alone cannot give it, because a BH can be played from the FH side and vice versa (pivots, round the corner). Needs where the ball lands (as the serve landing quadrant) or a light redirect mark. Left-handers flip. | Paul and coordinator | 2026-11-18 |
| Parked (Paul, 2026-11-18: likely too much mental load). Prototype test: capture placement during the contact phase. Instead of one contact tap, tap left or right for each contact so the tap gives contact plus side or corner (the serve tap gives where the server contacts the ball). Risk: live tapping is fast and result buttons during the rally were tried and dropped. | Paul | 2026-11-18 |
| Order Matches by last edited, once a change records an updated time. | Matches page plan | 2026-11-20 |
| Rename Tournament to Event on the Insights filter. | Matches page plan | 2026-11-20 |
