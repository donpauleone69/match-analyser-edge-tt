# End, then the opener

Ask where the ball ended, then which shot attacked first, then only the serve and the shots those answers leave open. The winner, an ace, and a receive you already named with a stroke are never asked. Spin, intent, and the middle of the rally stay uncollected, because a blank is safer than a guess.

## Sequence

Contacts are already marked. The server comes from the score. One tap answers and the next question appears. Nothing is lit in advance. Tap a saved line to change it. A new opener clears a stroke that no longer fits.

1. Where it ended: Winner, Net, Long, or Wide.
2. Which shot attacked first, from shot 2 on, plus None. A rally longer than five shows 2, 3, 4, 5, Later, and None. Later opens the rest and is not saved. One shot skips this step.
3. Serve length: Short, Half, or Long.
4. My receive, only when they served, the rally has three or more shots, and shot 2 was not the attack: Short or Long.
5. If a shot was named: Flick, Loop, Drive, or Smash, then forehand or backhand.
6. If the last shot is a different contact: its stroke, then its wing. No attack uses Push, Block, Chop, Lob. An earlier attack uses Block, Loop, Drive, Smash.

A five-shot rally with your third ball is seven taps. The same rally on their serve, if you did not attack the receive, is eight. An ace is two.

## What is stored, and why

- **Ended.** Winner, net, long, or wide. Visible, and it feeds winners and the miss reels.
- **Winner, inferred.** The last hitter won if it landed in. Otherwise the other player won.
- **Serve length.** Serves that won, and the ball the third ball came from. Half stays, because half-long is a real serve.
- **First attack, or none.** This places the receive and the third ball. None is a tap, so a skip cannot look like a push rally.
- **Opening stroke and wing.** A missed first attack is this shot being last, plus a miss.
- **Receive length, sometimes.** Short or long, only for your receive, and only when a stroke did not already describe it.
- **Last stroke and wing**, when the finish is not the opener.

Every other contact is a time only.

## Inferred or filtered

Server and hitter come from the score. Winner comes from the result. One shot has no first attack. If you attacked the receive, or it ended the point, the stroke is the receive. The opener filters the last stroke, so an attack and None cannot both be saved.

## Dropped

A per-shot offensive or defensive toggle, including a lit default. Forced and unforced, and a second miss reason. Intent, spin, high, heavy, initiative, placement, and pivot. Strokes on the middle shots. Their receive is not asked.

## The trade-off

Once someone has attacked, a chop or a lob finish is saved as a block. You also skip receive length when the stroke already says flick, push, or chop. In return a rally stays at eight taps or fewer, every tap is a choice, and serve, receive, the opener, and the ending are enough for those reels.
