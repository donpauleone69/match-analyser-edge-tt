# Reliable anchors, then selective detail

Interactive model: [Reliable rally tagging sequence](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-7c210afe-9d07-40db-82c4-34e629d311ff/2e12b4df-f38d-484b-80d6-961ab83196bb)

## Sequence

1. **End first.** Choose Clean winner, Error, or Not clear. Then tag the last shot as Attack or Control, followed by its filtered stroke and wing. Skip shot is a real answer that stores no shot detail.
2. **First attack next.** Tap its contact on the rally row, switch on No attack, or choose Can’t tell. If a shot is chosen, only attack strokes appear, then FH or BH.
3. **Serve pattern.** Ask length. Ask spin only for the player’s own serve, where it is normally known. Unsure stores no value.
4. **Receive pattern.** If shot 2 was the first attack, reuse that detail. If it came before the first attack, show only control strokes. Ask nothing twice.
5. **Review and save.** Show the few stored and inferred facts. Middle shots remain blank on purpose.

This uses the clearest event as an anchor, then follows the point from serve to receive. It is not fully shot by shot because asking about every middle contact adds taps and encourages guesses.

## Fields that earn a place

- **Winner and winner/error finish:** power the basic match summary and won/lost point reels. Winner is inferred from the last hitter plus winner/error, so it is not asked separately.
- **Last stroke and wing:** show which techniques finish points or break down. They create useful end-shot reels.
- **First attack contact, stroke and wing:** show who gained initiative and which opening succeeds or fails. This is a strong coaching and reel axis.
- **Serve length, plus known spin on own serves:** connect serve pattern to point outcome. Opponent spin is omitted because phone video often makes it guesswork.
- **Receive stroke and wing:** connect receive choice to first attack and point outcome. This supports the Receive insight rather than merely describing the rally.

## Inference and filtering

Contacts already provide shot count, order and hitter. Clean winner means the last hitter won; Error means the other player won. Shots before the selected first attack are control phase, without pretending their exact stroke is known. Attack or Control filters four strokes into one iPhone row. End, first-attack and receive details are reused whenever they refer to the same contact. A two-shot rally can infer first attack or No attack from the receive’s stroke family.

## Dropped

Forced versus unforced, miss direction, Safe versus Aggressive, High, Heavy, initiative, corner and general middle-shot detail are dropped. Their extra insight value is smaller than their ambiguity and tap cost. The saved video remains the evidence when a coach wants to inspect one of these moments.

## Main trade-off

This costs more taps than tagging only the end and first attack because serve and receive are included. In return it produces the smallest dependable set that can support Summary, Serve, Receive and Improve views. Explicit Not clear, Unsure and Skip choices mean uncertainty becomes missing data, never a guessed category.
