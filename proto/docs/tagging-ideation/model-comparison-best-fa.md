# Tagging models compared

Best + FA against the review list in `comparison.md`, using the later write-ups where they replace an earlier draft (Spine r4, Open end r3 and r4, Best of all). Canvas: [Tagging models compared](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-7c210afe-9d07-40db-82c4-34e629d311ff/4b40d60d-bb36-40e8-b609-fccff5e1d70a).

Count, every model: one tap per contact, one per required answer, plus any Save or Next. Not counted: fixes, review marks, stars, and optional questions. Tour mix, 80 rallies: 10, 14, 21, 10, 9, 5, 3, 8 at 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 10 shots (317 contacts).

Time is one estimate for all: 1.3 s watch per contact, a 2 s pause, 1.2 s per later tap. About 14 s a rally is about 18 min a match.

Best + FA is counted as built: v0.13.1 on PR #133 (commit `8d1307c`), the same as the plan's section 1. Order: last shot (Winner, Net, Long or Wide, Point to, last stroke), then serve, receive, third, shot 4 from 7 shots, then the other end shots. The optional "Who attacked first?" sits on the first end shot page from 8 shots and covers 5 to n-3, so a skip adds no tap and no wait. The last required stroke saves. Taps are `bestTaps` and the plan's built column in section 5: 3, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 at 1 to 7 shots, 19 at 10.

Length cells are a clean rally (serve landed). A service fault skips serve length on the models that ask it. That is a few rallies and is inside the average. Four beats, Four taps and Tag the point branch, so their length cells are the usual path and their average is the published mix plus contacts.

## Each model

**Spine r4.** Result, serve length, and a named stroke on shots 2, 3, 4 and the last two. Wing only on attacks. First attack is read from shots 2 to 4. Rating 8: the best facts per tap. The point is one button, with no contact check.

**Best + FA.** Serve length, wing and attack or control on shots 2, 3, shot 4 from 7 shots, and the last three, plus how the point ended and a winner checked against the contacts. Shots 5 to n-3 are blank from 8 shots up; the optional question says who attacked first there. Strokes are Attack or Control, with Counter read from an attack before it. An ace has no wing. Rating 8: a checked score and a full opening with shot 4, at one tap a rally more than Spine r4, which still names strokes.

**Open end r4.** Same shots as r3, a separate who-won tap, a serve-order check, where winners land, and who kept the attack at the end. Rating 7.5: the fullest fixed slots, at the same time as Best + FA. One missed contact and one extra can cancel and pass the check.

**Open end r3.** Wing and attack on shots 2, 3, 4 and the last three, serve length, and one tap for net, out, or where a winner landed. Rating 7: the same slots as r4, one tap faster, and a missed contact can award the point the wrong way.

**Best of all.** The Best + FA opening, and only the last stroke after that. Point to saves. Rating 7: the balls before the finish are blank, which is the 10 shot gap this plan closes.

**Four beats.** Result on every rally, then your serve, the first attack shot, a lane when you lose, and your wing when you hit last. Strokes are a push-then-topspin rule. Length cells are you serving and winning. Rating 6: the fastest pass, and several rates have no full set behind them.

**Bookend.** Last ball result, wing and stroke, plus the previous ball's stroke and lane. Rating 6: the closing pair is comparable, and the serve and the receive are missing once the rally is past two shots.

**Four taps.** A spot for the end, the serve and the receive, last-ball wing, and a live tap for the first attack. Length cells are the amateur row plus contacts. Rating 6: real placement, with the receive missing when the point ends there, and strokes inferred.

**Open end r2.** A score pass, then serve length and one attack row on shots 2, 3 and the last three. Length cells round a 0.4 zone tap. Rating 6: two passes, no wing on a push or a block, and a moving first-attack pick on long rallies. r3 replaces it.

**Tag the point.** Result, which shot attacked, serve length, opener and last stroke with wing. Your receive length only sometimes. Length cells are your serve. Rating 5: the right core, on a path that changes every rally, near 10 taps.

**Current.** Who won, six results, last stroke and wing, and the first attack on a second look. Rating 5: about 15 s, and the attack stroke sits on whichever shot you tap.

**Shot by shot.** Stroke and wing from the serve until two balls after the attack, then the last shot. Rating 4: about a third of the taps are Next on rows that are not stored.

**End first.** The same facts and the same taps as Shot by shot. The clip holds longer at the end of each shot. Rating 4: the hold does not remove the Next taps.

## Verdict

Best + FA wins on a score checked against the contacts, a wing on both attack and control, shots 2 to 4 and the last three, and no Save or Next. It loses to Spine r4 on stroke names, and to Open end r4 on where a winner landed. Same cost band as Open end r4 (about 14 s, about 18 min). It is the right main flow.

Changes:

1. Done in v0.13.1: shot 4 on rallies of 7 or more. One tap on 11 rallies of the mix, 9.4 to 9.5 a rally. The optional question now covers attacks after shot 4.
2. Still open: on the report, name the attack from the ball before it: flick after a short ball, topspin after a push. Counter after an attack is already shown. No new button.
3. Still open: when the result is Winner, one tap for where it landed: backhand, middle or forehand. About half of rallies. The previous shot already shows whether an error came off an attack, so forced or unforced adds less.
