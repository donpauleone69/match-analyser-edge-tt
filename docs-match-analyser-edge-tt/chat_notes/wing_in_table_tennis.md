# Wing in table tennis

_Chat notes — 2026-11-11_

Superseded by the living terminology note: [wing_placement_pivot_crossover.md](wing_placement_pivot_crossover.md).

Wing is the stroke, the side of the body: forehand (FH) or backhand (BH). Placement is the side of the table. Pivot is a forehand played from the backhand side.
